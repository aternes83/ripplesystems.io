How it worksSimply smart

Owners should not need a manual to enjoy their tub. Ripple keeps it to three steps, and the app takes it from there.

  1. 1Install

    A Ripple dealer wires the controller into the tub and fits the touch panel.

  2. 2Pair

    Stand at the tub, open the app, and it finds the spa over Bluetooth and sets up its Wi-Fi.

  3. 3Relax

    Heat, jets, lights and Away mode from anywhere, with alerts that say what to do.

FeaturesEverything at the tub, and in your pocket

Every setting lives in the app. Change it on the phone and the panel at the tub redraws itself within seconds.

  • Day & night touch panel

    A temperature you can read across the deck. Day mode is made for bright sun.

  • Alerts that say what to do

    Water ready, freeze protection, no flow, heater cutoff and spa offline, even with the app closed.

  • Smart scheduler

    Heat windows for off-peak electricity, with a lower hold temperature in between.

  • Eco & Away

    Eco saves energy day to day. Away keeps the tub cool while you travel, then heats it for your return.

  • Make it theirs

    Name the tub and every AUX button, show only what the tub has, and set run timers.

  • Updates over the air

    New features reach the panel with no service call. The app updates through the App Store.

  • Panel lock

    Lock the panel from the app. The temperature, Eco and Away are the app's alone, and jets started at the tub stop after 10 minutes.

  • Circulation pump

    One, two or three jet pumps, with or without a circulation pump that runs only while heating or around the clock.

  • Smart load sharing

    With every jet on high, the heater pauses instead of tripping a 50 A breaker, and picks up again as soon as a pump stops.

Bright by day. Easy at night.

A 4.3-inch glass touch panel at the tub, with big targets for wet hands and two looks you switch from the app.

  • Tap the bar on the right edge and the AUX buttons slide in, named by the owner.
  • Fewer pumps? AUX buttons take the empty Jet 2 and Jet 3 spots.
  • Hold Light to step a color-changing bulb through its shows.
  • Dims itself after 30 minutes untouched. A tap wakes it, and that tap does nothing else.
