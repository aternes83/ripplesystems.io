Support

Help with your Ripple spa controller and the Ripple Spa app. If your question is not answered here, email us at support@ripplesystems.io.

Getting started

  1. Download Ripple Spa from the App Store.
  2. Stand near your spa with Bluetooth turned on.
  3. Open the app and tap Set Up My Spa. Your phone finds the spa and sets everything up.
  4. If the spa is not on Wi-Fi yet, pick your home network and enter its password.

The spa needs a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network. Most home routers provide one alongside 5 GHz.

Try it without a spa

On the first screen, tap No spa yet? Try the demo. The app runs on a simulated tub, and every control works. Tap Exit in the blue banner to leave the demo.

Using the app

  • Temperature: drag the ring on the dial, or use the + and − buttons.
  • Pumps and light: tap a tile to turn it on or off. Pump 1 has Low and High, or is simply on and off when your spa has a circulation pump.
  • Eco and Away: Eco holds a lower temperature to save energy. Away keeps the tub cool while you travel and heats it back up for your return.
  • Schedule: the calendar icon sets heat windows, for example during off-peak electricity hours.
  • Settings: the gear icon names your spa and its extras, says which pumps it has (including a circulation pump that runs only when heating, or 24/7), sets run timers, and chooses the panel's day or night look.
  • Panel lock: turn on Lock the panel in Settings and only the app can change the temperature, Eco and Away. Pumps turned on at the spa run for 10 minutes. A yellow padlock shows on the panel while it is locked.

Troubleshooting

The app says "Spa offline". The spa cannot reach the internet. Check that the spa has power and that your Wi-Fi is working. If you changed your Wi-Fi name or password, go to Settings → Pair with a spa, or change its Wi-Fi.

Setup cannot find the spa. Make sure Bluetooth is on, stand within a few feet of the spa's touch panel, and check that the panel is powered. If iPhone asks, allow Bluetooth for Ripple in iPhone Settings.

Alerts are not arriving. Turn on notifications for Ripple in iPhone Settings → Notifications, then check your alert choices in the app under Settings → Notifications.

The panel is dim. After 30 minutes without a touch, the panel dims itself. Tap anywhere to bring it back. That tap only wakes the panel, so it will not start a pump or change the temperature. An alert wakes the panel too.

The dial on the panel will not turn. The panel is locked: look for the yellow padlock at the top. Change the temperature in the app, or turn off Lock the panel in the app's Settings.

The jets stopped after 10 minutes. With the panel locked, pumps turned on at the spa run for 10 minutes; the tile counts down the minutes left. Pumps turned on from the app keep their usual run time.

The heater pauses while all the jets are on. With Pump 1 on High and Pumps 2 and 3 on, the spa would draw more than a 50 A breaker allows, so it pauses the heater. Heating picks up again as soon as one pump stops. This is normal, not a fault.

The temperature reads wrong. Use Settings → Calibrate temperature sensor, and compare against a thermometer in the water.

What the alerts mean

AlertWhat happenedWhat to do
No water flowA pump was running but no water was moving. The spa turned off its pumps and heater.Check the water level and clean or replace the filter.
High-limit cutoffThe heater's safety cutoff tripped.Let the water cool. If your heater has a reset button, press it.
Over-temperatureThe water is hotter than it should be.Stay out of the water. Contact your dealer if it continues.
Emergency stopThe emergency stop was pressed.Release it once it is safe.
Sensor faultThe water temperature sensor is disconnected or out of range.Contact your dealer.
Freeze protectionThe water is near freezing. The spa is heating to protect the pipes.No action needed. Check the cover is on.

Updates

The app updates through the App Store. The spa controller updates over the air: when new firmware is ready, the app shows it under Settings → Controller firmware, and you choose when to install it.

Safety

Your spa's built-in safety switches keep working without the internet. Heating and freeze protection run on the controller itself. Installation and electrical work must be done by a licensed electrician or a Ripple dealer.
