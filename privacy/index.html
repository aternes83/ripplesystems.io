Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

Ripple Systems LLC ("Ripple", "we") makes the Ripple Spa app and the Ripple spa controller. We collect only what is needed to run your spa and send its alerts. We do not track you, show ads, or sell or share your information for marketing.

What this policy covers

This policy covers the Ripple Spa app for iPhone, the Ripple spa controller (the touch panel and control board in your hot tub), and the online services that connect them. It does not cover other companies' services, such as Apple's, which have their own policies.

No account needed

You do not create an account. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts or photos.

What stays on your phone

The app stores its settings on your iPhone: the paired spa's connection details, your spa's nickname, your schedule and your alert choices. It keeps recent water temperatures in memory for up to 3 hours to draw the chart, and clears them when the app closes. Deleting the app deletes all of this.

What your spa sends to us

Your spa controller connects through your home Wi-Fi to Ripple's message service. It sends the spa's status: water temperature, target temperature, which equipment is on, Eco and Away mode, faults, firmware version, and whether it is online.

The service keeps the latest status so the app can show it when you open it. Each new status replaces the last; we do not keep a history. Commands you give in the app, such as turning on the jets, travel the same way. Each spa has its own login, so no one else's app can see or control your spa.

Alerts

To send alerts when the app is closed, the app gives our alert service:

  • the push token Apple issues to your iPhone for this app (a random identifier, not your phone number or Apple ID);
  • your spa's ID, which comes from the controller's hardware;
  • your spa's nickname, so each alert names your spa;
  • which alerts you turned on, and whether your heater has a reset button;
  • a code that proves your phone was paired with that spa.

Alerts are delivered by Apple Push Notification service. We remove your phone's registration when you turn alerts off in the app, or when Apple tells us the token is no longer valid.

Wi-Fi setup over Bluetooth

The app uses Bluetooth only to pair with your spa controller during setup. It sends your Wi-Fi network name and password directly from your phone to the controller, which stores them to join your network. Your Wi-Fi password is not sent to Ripple. The app also gives the controller your time zone, so its clock shows local time.

Update checks

The app downloads a small file that lists the latest app and firmware versions, hosted on GitHub. The controller may check Ripple's update service for new firmware. Like any web request, these reveal your internet (IP) address to the server that answers.

Technical logs

Our servers record technical logs, such as connection times, IP addresses and errors, to keep the service running and secure. Logs are kept for 30 days and then deleted.

Who we share information with

We do not sell your information. We share it only with:

  • Service providers that run our services for us: our cloud hosting provider (Oracle Cloud), Apple (to deliver alerts) and GitHub (to host the update file). They may use it only to provide their service.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.
  • A new owner, if Ripple's business is sold or merged. This policy would continue to apply to your information.

Security

Everything between the app, the controller and our services travels encrypted (TLS). Each spa signs in with its own credentials, and a phone must prove it was paired with a spa before it receives that spa's alerts.

Your choices

  • Turn alerts off in the app, or in iPhone Settings.
  • Delete the app to remove everything it stored on your phone.
  • Email us to delete what our services hold about your spa, or to ask what we hold. Include your spa's ID, shown in the app under Settings → Advanced → Spa ID.

Children

Ripple Spa is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
